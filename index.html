Why It Matters

The industry is growing
faster than its
oversight.

GLP-1 medications — Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound — have fundamentally changed what patients expect from health and wellness practices. Medspas, IV clinics, and hormone therapy providers are multiplying to meet that demand. The question of who's actually qualified to administer these treatments is one most patients never think to ask.

The Opportunity
A new patient base, fast

GLP-1 medications have brought an entirely new category of patient into aesthetic and wellness practices. Practices that added GLP-1 services in 2024 saw revenue grow by an average of 9% — compared to a 2% decline at those that didn't. Weight loss treatments are now the fourth largest non-surgical aesthetics segment in the US. The growth is real, and it's not slowing down.

The Gap
Growth without guardrails

With that growth comes a problem. New practices are opening constantly to meet demand, and patients have no easy way to know whether a provider is properly licensed, credentialed, or in good standing. There's no central standard, no public badge, no quick answer. That's the gap BeautyJar and BackBeam were built to address — on the patient side and the practice side.

What we
built.

Wenley Tech is the parent company behind BeautyJar and BackBeam Network — two platforms focused on provider verification and compliance in the health and wellness space.

BeautyJar helps patients find providers who have been checked and verified. BackBeam helps practices stay current on credentialing and maintain the documentation to prove it.

Both platforms are focused on the same fast-growing space: GLP-1 clinics, medspas, hormone therapy practices, and medical aesthetics providers across the US.
